The Russian House in Berlin

An EU-sanctioned Russian state agency continues to operate a major cultural centre in a 29,000 m² building in central Berlin – despite EU sanctions and Russia’s continuing war of aggression against Ukraine.

Key political decisions
Deadline: 6 December 2026
One agreement can now be terminated
The 2011 operational agreement can be terminated by ordinary notice. Germany can give written notice by 6 December 2026 to end it on 6 June 2027.
Official assessment · July 2026
France: Cover for intelligence services
A French expulsion order describes the Russian House as a cover for Russian intelligence services. This puts substantial pressure on the cultural classification underlying Germany’s restraint.
