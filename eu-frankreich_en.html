France country file

Cooperation suspended, treaty in force

France is the clearest case of a treaty-based cultural mission attached to an embassy: the 1992 intergovernmental agreement reciprocally establishes a French centre in Moscow and a Russian one in Paris. France suspended state cooperation – but did not terminate the agreement and did not close the Maison russe. It was open in July 2026.

Active official location State cooperation suspended

Four levers that are not the same thing

France separates cleanly what is elsewhere conflated: programme cooperation suspended, financial sanctions enforced, diplomatic personnel status affected – but the cultural agreement not terminated. The much-cited freezing of the Maison russe’s accounts derives solely from Russian reporting and is not confirmed by any French official source. Reading rule: “Open” means the statement is not documented in the public sources examined. It is not proof of the contrary. EU listing, national enforcement, political non-cooperation, treaty termination and actual closure of operations are assessed separately.

Legal basis

The legal basis is the intergovernmental agreement of 12 November 1992, in force since 8 June 1994 and published in France by Décret No 94-985. It creates two mirror institutions and attaches the Paris centre to the Russian embassy.

https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000713310

The agreement gives the sending state the legal capacity required to operate the centre. It therefore evidences precisely no legal person of the house independent of the Russian state. That distinction matters for questions of ownership, accounts and enforcement.

https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000713310

According to the official sources examined, France has not terminated the agreement. What is documented is the suspension of state cooperation – a political and administrative act, not one that ends the treaty.

https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr

Measures since 2022

In April 2022 France expelled several dozen Russian nationals holding diplomatic status, citing activity against French security interests. No connection to the Maison russe was drawn.

https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr

The official Registre national des gels carries Rossotrudnichestvo under entry 4897. This confirms implementation of the EU listing in the French enforcement register, but not any specific match against an account of the Maison russe.

https://gels-avoirs.dgtresor.gouv.fr

A freezing of accounts from late 2024 and a partial release in 2025 were asserted solely in Russian reporting. Neither the French economics ministry nor the sanctions authority published any case-specific confirmation. Scope, addressee and legal basis remain unconfirmed; the claim is carried here as an indication only, not as an established finding.

https://iz.ru

Assessment

The most useful model for the German debate

France offers the model the German discussion most lacks: suspend cooperation, leave the treaty in place, let the centre continue operating. All three decisions were taken separately and are separately documented.

That imposes a requirement of language: programme cooperation, financial sanctions enforcement, diplomatic personnel status and termination of a cultural agreement must be clearly distinguished. Reporting the French suspension as a “closure” describes a different event.

The French agreement also shows which treaty clauses a German assessment would have to construe individually: legal personality, fees, taxes and customs, personnel status, and the termination clause.
