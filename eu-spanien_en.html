Spain country file

Legally capable – but expressly not inviolable

Spain is among the most thoroughly documented cases in this research in legal terms. The 2001 agreement reciprocally governs the Instituto Cervantes in Moscow and the Russian centre in Madrid, confers on the centres the rights of legal persons, places them under host-state law – and expressly states that the premises are not diplomatically inviolable. That makes Spain the most useful legal comparator for the German debate.

Three dividing lines drawn by the Spanish treaty

First: legal capacity is not diplomatic inviolability – the treaty expressly excludes it for the premises. Second: privileges depend on function and accreditation; only accredited management can receive diplomatic privileges. Third: sanctions enforcement and treaty termination remain separate – Spain can enforce EU asset rules without terminating the agreement. Reading rule: “Open” means the statement is not documented in the public sources examined. It is not proof of the contrary. EU listing, national enforcement, political non-cooperation, treaty termination and actual closure of operations are assessed separately.

Legal basis

The centre agreement of 15 November 2001 is the most detailed treaty text in this collection. It confers on the centres the rights of legal persons, places them in principle under host-state law, permits non-profit fees and governs personnel and taxes.

https://www.boe.es

Decisive is an express negative provision: the premises are not diplomatically inviolable. This distinguishes Spain sharply from Austria, where the centre is notified as part of the embassy.

https://www.boe.es

The 2007 protocol, fully in force since 10 March 2009, specified above all education, tax and control matters. Whether the wording of the agreement establishes a separate legal person or merely grants legal capacity for operations is a matter for case-by-case legal construction.

https://www.boe.es

Measures since 2022

On 5 April 2022 Spain expelled some 25 Russian diplomats and embassy staff, citing security interests and the crimes in Bucha and Mariupol. Whether staff or management of the house were among them is open.

https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es

Royal Decree-Law 9/2022 of 26 April 2022 created national mechanisms for implementing EU asset freezes. It is general in nature and not specific to the house. No termination of the treaty and no enforcement act specific to the centre were found.

https://www.boe.es

Reports by OCCRP and El Periódico concerning a “Casa de Rusia” in Alicante, Pravfond funding and a complaint discontinued in 2025 are not attributed to the Madrid centre. The reports name a different city and management; no organisational, operator or contractual evidence equating the two was found.

Assessment

The best checklist for the German debate

Because of its detailed treaty, Spain is the strongest legal comparator in this collection. Three points are particularly useful: legal capacity is not diplomatic inviolability; privileges depend on function and accreditation; sanctions enforcement and treaty termination remain separate.

The last point cuts both ways: Spain can enforce EU asset rules without automatically terminating the centre agreement – and conversely the termination clause is no substitute for a concrete freeze.

From this follows a checklist for Germany: establish separately the precise legal person, ownership and control, title to the premises, accreditation, tax status, national enforcement acts and independent contractual termination options. The Spanish treaty shows that a centre agreement can regulate these questions expressly – including to the Russian side’s disadvantage.

Open questions

  • Were staff or management of the house among those expelled in April 2022?
  • Who owns the premises at Calle Alcalá 61, and what does “provision by the mission” mean today?
  • Which accounts and revenues does the centre itself hold, and what freeze or authorisation decisions exist?
  • Which management personnel are accredited as diplomats under Article VIII of the agreement?
  • How regular is public operation in August 2026? The site advertises on-site offerings, but no dated event at the house was found for 2026.
