Conduct of Authorities

Police & Protest

Law enforcement deployments targeting peaceful protests often occur swiftly and with substantial resources, whereas protective measures for the demonstrators have repeatedly failed to materialize. This documented imbalance is increasingly becoming the subject of legal challenges.

🚔 The Documented Imbalance
  • At least 33 police deployments were registered in the immediate vicinity of the Russian House in connection with the protests during 2024. Although the identity of the party calling the police in individual instances is not officially documented, reports indicate that the deployments were directed almost exclusively against the protester. t-online, May 2025
  • Over 100 criminal complaints were filed by the Russian House administration against Henry Lindemeier. In three cases, penal orders (Strafbefehle) were issued, which are currently being challenged legally. Süddeutsche Zeitung, December 2025
  • In one criminal complaint alleging incitement to hatred (Volksverhetzung), the State Criminal Police Office (LKA) translated the played Russian song lyrics and reached a definitive finding: The lyrics did not constitute incitement. Nevertheless, the investigative proceedings were kept open for several months. t-online, May 2025
  • By May 2025, according to media reports, none of the allegations raised by the venue's management regarding verbal insults or threats had been substantiated. In the case of complaints regarding noise disturbance, the police refrained from conducting official sound level measurements and did not state any concrete limit. t-online, May 2025
“When a noise disturbance is reported, up to three patrol cars are sometimes dispatched to the Russian House. Yet, when my client was specifically threatened, the dispatch center claimed no officers were available.”
Attorney Christian Wolf – t-online, May 2025
⚖️ The Unlawful Arrest – December 18, 2024
  • Henry Lindemeier was arrested and handcuffed outside the Russian House before being taken to a police detention facility.
  • This was followed by a four-hour deprivation of liberty as well as extensive booking and processing procedures.
  • The charges cited were alleged insults and noise disturbances from previous weeks. Both remained entirely unsubstantiated by police records.
  • Following a written apology from the Berlin police, who sought to consider the matter settled, Lindemeier's attorneys filed a lawsuit.
  • During the initiated action for a declaratory judgment of an unlawful police measure (Fortsetzungsfeststellungsklage), the Berlin police formally conceded: The entire operation and the deprivation of liberty were unlawful. t-online, May 2025
  • On the day prior to this unlawful arrest, Lindemeier himself had contacted the police after being heavily threatened outside the building (“You will get a knife in your back sooner or later”). In that instance, no patrol car was dispatched to the scene. t-online, May 2025
🎭 The BSW Incident – May 2025
  • During a wreath-laying ceremony by BSW party leader Amira Mohamed Ali at the Soviet War Memorial in Tiergarten, Henry Lindemeier was physically pushed aside by several men from her entourage.
  • When Lindemeier loudly called for the nearby police to intervene, the officers instead issued him a dispersal order (Platzverweis) and demanded he leave the area, while taking no action against his aggressors.
  • A police spokesperson explained the decision: “The group perceived the gentleman's appearance as a disruption to their silent commemoration.” t-online, May 2025
  • Lindemeier has announced his intention to have this police intervention judicially reviewed as well.
📅 December 9, 2025 – Counter-Event Displaces the Protest
  • For this date, an official counter-demonstration was registered directly in front of the Russian House under the motto “Do not let yourself be provoked!”. The mobilization relied on a targeted appeal by the association “Integral e.V.” in Russian-language Telegram groups.
  • Lindemeier was only notified by the Berlin police via email the night before regarding the restrictions imposed on his ongoing permanent vigil.
  • Citing the registered counter-event, the police prohibited Lindemeier from holding his vigil at his established location.
  • While approximately 20 police officers secured the entrance area, Lindemeier's protest was prevented from taking place. In the aftermath, the police even classified a calm conversation between the activist and his supporters as an “unregistered assembly” and threatened criminal charges. Süddeutsche Zeitung, December 2025
“Whose freedom is the Berlin police actually protecting here?”
Henry Lindemeier – Süddeutsche Zeitung, December 2025

Formally conceded during legal proceedings

The Arrest of December 18, 2024, Was Unlawful

In the legal dispute within the framework of an action for a declaratory judgment, the Berlin police have officially conceded that the arrest and subsequent detention of Henry Lindemeier lacked any legal basis. In the view of the affected party's legal counsel, the rapid succession of these documented incidents strongly suggests that this does not represent an isolated administrative failure, but rather a systematic pattern in the treatment of critics of the state-run Russian institution.
