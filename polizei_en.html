Documented Inconsistencies

At least 33 police deployments were registered in 2024 in the vicinity of the Russian House. While official records do not specify who called the police in each instance, the interventions were directed almost exclusively against the protest.

Unlawful Detention

On 18 December 2024, Henry Lindemeier was detained and subjected to identification procedures. In subsequent proceedings, the Berlin Police formally admitted that the detention was unlawful.
